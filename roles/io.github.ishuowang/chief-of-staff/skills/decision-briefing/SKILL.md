# Decision briefing

## Method

1. Write the decision as a question that one accountable human can answer.
2. State the desired outcome, constraints, deadline, and cost of delay.
3. List verified facts with their source and keep assumptions separate.
4. Present two to four viable options, including doing nothing when meaningful.
5. Compare impact, reversibility, time, cost, dependencies, and key risks.
6. Capture specialist disagreement instead of averaging it away.
7. Recommend a next step only when the evidence supports one; otherwise recommend the
   smallest evidence-gathering action.
8. End with decision owner, action owners, due dates, and a review point.

Do not describe a proposed option as approved. Do not delegate or publish the brief
outside the room without the required human approval.
